# Bolivian Rainbow — Cultivation Notes (Indoor Mini-Greenhouse)

**Habit & size.** _Capsicum annuum_ cultivar from Bolivia with a naturally **compact, bushy** habit.
Typical indoor height **45–60 cm** (occasionally up to 90 cm in ideal conditions), with a similar
spread. Features dark green to **purple-tinged foliage** and small **purple star-shaped flowers**.
Grown both as an ornamental and a culinary chilli.

**Fruit & colour.** The defining trait is the **multicolour display**: small (1–3 cm), cone or
teardrop-shaped pods that ripen through **purple → cream → yellow → orange → red**, with all colours
often present on the plant at the same time. Fruits point upward and resemble small coloured bulbs.
Heat is **medium, around 10,000–30,000 SHU**, similar to cayenne or tabasco.

**Temperature.** As a warm-climate annual, it performs at common room temperatures. Best day range
**21–28°C**; nights ideally a little cooler at **16–21°C**, though it does not depend on a large
night drop the way tomatoes do. Germination is fastest at **24–29°C** soil temperature. The variety
tolerates warm conditions up to about 30°C without significant stress, making it suitable for warm
indoor setups.

**Light.** Seedlings **14–16 h/day**, vegetative **12–16 h**, flowering **12–14 h**. Keep LEDs close
enough to limit internode stretch while avoiding leaf scorch. Full-spectrum LED works well. A minimum
of strong light for 12+ hours is needed for compact growth and good fruit colour.

**Humidity & airflow.** Target **40–65% RH**. Unlike the superhot _Capsicum chinense_ varieties, this
_annuum_ **does not require high humidity** and handles dry indoor air well. Gentle, consistent
airflow strengthens stems and reduces fungal pressure. Sustained high humidity (>75%) indoors can
favor disease, so ventilate.

**Watering.** Water when the **top 1–2 cm** of substrate are dry. The variety is **somewhat
drought-tolerant** but lack of water during fruiting reduces yield. It is **sensitive to wet soil
and root rot**, so allow dry-back between waterings and never keep the substrate saturated. In cool
or low-light conditions, reduce watering frequency.

**Nutrition.** Begin mild feeding after true leaves appear. Indicative EC bands: **seedling 1.0–1.2**,
**vegetative 1.2–1.6**, **flowering slightly leaner on nitrogen**. Aim **pH 6.0–6.8**. Peppers are
moderately hungry plants that need **phosphorus and potassium** but **not too much nitrogen**—excess
nitrogen produces lush foliage but few fruits.

**Containers & training.** Use **free-draining** mixes (see _Soil Mix Basics_). Pot volume **3–10 L**
per plant; for a compact indoor plant 3–5 L is usually sufficient. Step up pot size gradually.
Optional **light pinching** during vegetative growth promotes bushiness; avoid heavy pruning near
flowering. Spacing **45–60 cm** for good airflow.

**Pollination.** Self-fertile. In still indoor air, pollination can be poor; gentle airflow from a
fan usually suffices, or lightly shake the flower clusters.

**Timeline example (indoor, controlled environment).**

- **Week 0:** Transplant seedling into container.
- **Weeks 1–4:** Vegetative growth under 14–16 h light; build canopy; light pinch for bushiness.
- **Weeks 5–8:** First flowers appear; reduce nitrogen, raise potassium.
- **Weeks 8–14:** Fruit set and colour transition; harvest as pods reach desired colour.

**Common issues & quick fixes.**

- **Leggy seedlings:** increase photoperiod toward 16 h; reduce lamp distance; add airflow.
- **Blossom drop:** avoid temperature swings and overwatering; allow modest dry-back; keep nitrogen moderate.
- **Root rot / drooping despite watering:** overwatering in wet soil; improve drainage, allow dry-back.
- **Yellow leaves:** most often overwatering; less often underwatering, nutrient deficiency, or pests.
- **Spider mites:** fine webbing and stippled leaves; raise humidity slightly, use sticky traps, inspect undersides of leaves.
- **Fungus gnats:** let the top layer dry more; consider a sand/perlite cap; use sticky traps.

**Harvest.** Pods can be picked at any colour stage, but leaving them to ripen fully shows the
characteristic rainbow effect. **Frequent harvest** keeps production continuous. Handle and wash
hands after picking, as the pods carry medium heat.

**Safety bounds (policy reminders).** Keep advice within **10–35°C** and **6–18 h light**; present
deviations as **warnings** with brief rationale.
